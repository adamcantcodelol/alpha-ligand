THE HONEST BIT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The checklist score is feature coverage plus a size check. It is
NOT an affinity. The library screen cannot look at shape, and a
molecule with perfect chemistry can still be unable to enter the
pocket. Only the docking step (dock_ligands.py) puts a molecule
inside the protein, and even a good docking score is a weak
predictor of real binding. Treat the output as a shortlist worth
testing, not a prediction.

This run looked at the FIRST 250,000 rows of the library, not a
random sample of it and not all of it. ChEMBL rows are roughly
in order of when the compound entered the database, so a
capped scan is a historical slice, not a representative one.
Use --max-scan 0 to screen the whole collection.
